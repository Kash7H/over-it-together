# Over It Together: How to Play

1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click `OverItTogether.exe` and wait for the game to open. Keep the small window open while you play.
3. Accept your friend's Steam invite.

## In game

- **Esc** opens the menu: Multiplayer, Race, Practice, Settings and Controls.
- The top left shows the race clock, everyone's progress, and split times.

## Help

**"Windows protected your PC"**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Playing without the mod**: launch Getting Over It from Steam as normal.

**Your save**: races and practice never change your own climb unless you click **Keep this climb**.
